## Hook

A long gallery of armour stands, crossed swords and fine carpets. At the far end, on a plinth, is the thing you came for.

## Setup

The gallery is 70 feet long and 20 feet wide. The plinth is at the far end.
Everything in the roster is here, standing still as furniture.
The floor is polished stone. A carved frieze of closed mouths runs round the walls.
Nothing reacts when the party walks in quietly.

## Twist

The guardians hear; they do not see. They wake for sound and nothing else.
Each round, any creature that moves more than half its speed, wears heavy armour, casts a spell with a verbal component, or speaks above a whisper makes a Dexterity (Stealth) check against the DC. On a failure, the nearest sleeping guardian wakes and attacks the source of the noise.
Any fighting is loud: it wakes one more guardian at the end of each round.
A destroyed guardian reassembles on its stand one minute later. They cannot be cleared; only passed.
A waking guardian with no sound to follow returns to its stand after one round of silence.
Lifting the prize rings a chime in the plinth unless something of similar weight replaces it (Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) against the DC). The chime wakes everything.
Reveal: the frieze of closed mouths. An Intelligence (Investigation) check against the DC also notices that the armour has no eye-slits.

## Terrain and tactics

Woken guardians attack the loudest creature, not the nearest.
A thrown object makes a noise somewhere else: guardians go there for one round.
Magical silence makes the whole room trivial. Let it.

## Ways out without a fight

The whole encounter can be done without a single attack: move slowly, strip the noisy armour, swap the weight.
Distract with thrown coins, then walk out while the guardians investigate.

## Ripple

Prize taken silently: nobody knows the vault was opened, and another table may be sent to guard an empty plinth.
Prize taken loudly: the chime was heard upstairs, and the owner knows exactly when it happened.
Party driven off: the vault's keepers add a lock, and the next attempt is harder.

## Reskin notes

A sleeping dragon's hoard, a library with a fierce librarian, a nursery of sleeping basilisk young: any place where noise is the enemy.
Keep the rule that the guardians cannot be beaten for good, or the puzzle becomes a fight.
