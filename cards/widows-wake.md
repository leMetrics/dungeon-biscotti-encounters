## Hook

A widow in black stops you at the tavern door. Her husband's pallbearers have not come, the grave is dug, and she will pay four strong strangers to carry him up the hill before sundown.

## Setup

The coffin lies on trestles in the tavern's back room. It is heavy and nailed shut.
The walk to the hilltop graveyard takes 10 minutes through the whole village.
The widow is brisk, dry-eyed, and in a hurry. She pays 5 gp a bearer, half now.
On the route: a moneylender with two clerks at the well, a priest at the lychgate, and a great many curious neighbours.

## Twist

The husband is alive in the coffin. He owes the moneylender more than the farm is worth, and a dead man's debts die with him here.
The plan: be buried shallow, be dug up after dark, and leave for the coast.
The party is carrying the trick through the village without knowing it.
Three moments test it. Each is one check against the DC by whoever handles it:
the moneylender demands the coffin be opened "to pay respects";
the priest wants to say the long rite, an hour, and the man has air for half that;
a stumble on the hill, and a muffled curse from inside.
The party needs 3 successes before 2 failures to get him in the ground unexposed. They can also choose not to.
Reveal: a Wisdom (Insight) check against the DC on the widow, air holes drilled under the handles, or the coffin shifting its own weight on the stairs.

## Terrain and tactics

The widow lies well but not twice in a row.
The moneylender is right and unpleasant. The husband is wrong and likeable.
If exposed, nobody draws a weapon. It becomes an argument in the street.

## Ways out without a fight

There is no fight. The outcomes are: carry it through, expose it, or broker a deal.
A deal the moneylender accepts: the farm's next harvest, signed by a man who is officially alive.

## Ripple

Buried and gone: the moneylender takes his loss out on every other debtor in the valley, including people other tables know.
Exposed: the husband goes to the debtors' cells, and the widow remembers the party's faces.
Deal brokered: the village tells the story for years, with the party in it.

## Reskin notes

The "body" can be a deserter, a runaway bride, or a relic being smuggled past a toll.
Change who is in the right to suit your table: make the lender honest and the debt a gambling one.
