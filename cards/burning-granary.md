## Hook

Smoke pours from the village granary. Robed figures bar the doors from outside, and someone inside is shouting.

## Setup

The granary is a 40-foot timber hall with a loft. The village's winter grain is inside.
Two villagers are trapped on the ground floor.
The attackers hold the yard and the main doors. A side hatch is unguarded but jammed: Strength check against the DC.
Fire starts in the east corner. At the end of each round it takes one more 10-foot section.
A creature that starts its turn in a burning section takes the hazard damage.

## Twist

The fire is not the plan. The loft is thick with flour dust.
When the fire reaches the loft, at the end of round 5, the dust explodes: every creature inside or within 20 feet makes a Dexterity save against the DC, taking three times the hazard damage on a failure, half on a success. The granary is gone.
The attackers know. From round 4 they stop fighting and run.
Stopping it: shut the loft hatch (one action, on the ground floor ladder) to buy 2 rounds, or throw open both loft shutters (two actions, up top) to clear the dust for good.
Reveal: an Intelligence (Investigation) or Wisdom (Survival) check against the DC notices the white haze under the roof. Anyone raised on a farm knows at once.

## Terrain and tactics

Smoke fills the hall from round 2: lightly obscured inside.
Attackers fight in the yard and avoid going in.
Track the round out loud. Do not say why.

## Ways out without a fight

Ignore the yard: force the side hatch, pull the villagers out, and deal with the loft.
A Charisma (Intimidation) check against the DC at the start of round 3 makes the weaker attackers bolt early.

## Ripple

Granary saved: the village feeds travellers through winter and remembers who did it.
Granary lost, villagers saved: bread prices double at every table's next market.
Explosion with people inside: the village blames outsiders, the party included.

## Reskin notes

Any building with a hidden second danger works: a distillery, a fireworks store, a mine head with firedamp.
Swap the attackers for a rival family or an insurance scheme for a low-magic tone.
