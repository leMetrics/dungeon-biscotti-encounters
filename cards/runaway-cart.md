## Hook

A supply cart thunders down the hill road with no driver and no horses, raiders loping alongside it. The road ends in a hairpin above a ravine.

## Setup

The party starts 60 feet from the cart, on the road or the slope above.
The cart moves 60 feet at initiative count 0 each round. It reaches the hairpin at the end of round 5 and goes over.
The raiders cut the horses loose by mistake. Half of them ride or run beside the cart; half harry the party.
Boarding the moving cart: Dexterity (Acrobatics) or Strength (Athletics) against the DC. On a failure, take the hazard damage and fall prone.

## Twist

Winning the fight does nothing. The cart is the encounter.
Three things stop it. Each takes an action from someone on board or beside it, and a check against the DC:
the brake lever (Strength), which is jammed;
a wedge or body under the wheel (the helper takes the hazard damage);
steering it into the bank (Wisdom (Animal Handling) or a vehicle tool).
Two successes slow it to 30 feet a round. Three stop it.
The raiders want the cargo whole. If the party is clearly trying to stop the cart, the raiders stop attacking them and help: one raider success counts.
From round 3, knocking comes from inside the largest crate. Someone is in there.
Reveal: say at the top of every round how far the hairpin is. Mention the knocking in round 3, whoever is listening.

## Terrain and tactics

The slope is difficult terrain uphill, normal downhill.
Mounted raiders keep pace with the cart; raiders on foot fall behind after round 2.
If the cart goes over, everyone aboard takes five times the hazard damage.

## Ways out without a fight

Ignore the raiders entirely and stop the cart.
Shout a truce: Charisma (Persuasion) against the DC makes the raiders help from round 1.

## Ripple

Cart saved, cargo delivered: the town gets its supplies, and prices hold.
Cart saved, raiders take half: the raiders eat this winter and do not raid the next table's road.
Cart lost: the town goes short, and whoever was in the crate is missed by someone important.

## Reskin notes

Use a loose barge heading for a weir, a mine cart, or a festival float rolling toward the crowd.
The person in the crate is yours to choose: a stowaway, a smuggled witness, a sleeping heir. Keep them setting-neutral here.
