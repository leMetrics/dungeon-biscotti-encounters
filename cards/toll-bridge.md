## Hook

A rope bars an old stone bridge. Armed strangers want a toll for a crossing that was free last week.

## Setup

The bridge is 60 feet long and 10 feet wide, with no rails, 15 feet above a fast river.
The leader stands mid-span. Half the gang waits on the far bank with bows.
The toll is 5 gp a head. They will haggle down to 2.
The gang walks softly and never raises a voice on the stones.

## Twist

The bridge is alive and asleep. The gang found out by accident and keeps quiet on it.
Loud noise wakes it: thunder damage, a shout, a horn, or any round in which three or more creatures fight on the span.
When it wakes, it shrugs at the start of each round. Every creature on it makes a Dexterity save against the level DC. On a failure it falls prone; on a failure by 5 or more it drops into the river and takes the hazard damage.
One full round of silence puts it back to sleep.
Reveal: a Wisdom (Perception) check against the DC notices the gang's felt-wrapped boots and whispering.

## Terrain and tactics

The span is difficult terrain while the bridge is awake.
Archers on the far bank have half cover behind a cart.
The gang flees to the banks the moment the bridge stirs, and fights on from there.

## Ways out without a fight

Pay the toll, or haggle with a Charisma (Persuasion) check against the DC.
Point out, quietly, that you know why they whisper. The leader lets you pass rather than risk a shout.
Ford the river 200 yards upstream: Strength (Athletics) against the DC, or take the hazard damage and lose 10 minutes.

## Ripple

If the bridge was woken: travellers at other tables hear the crossing "walked off in the night" and the road is cut.
If the gang was paid or left in place: tolls are still charged, and prices rise in the next town.
If the gang was driven off quietly: the bridge sleeps on and nobody else learns its secret.

## Reskin notes

Any choke point works: a causeway, a rope ferry, a mountain ledge that is a giant's shoulder.
Swap the gang for local militia collecting an unlawful tax to make talking the better option.
