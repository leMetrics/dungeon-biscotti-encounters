## Hook

The trapdoor slams overhead and a bolt slides home. Somewhere in the dark, water starts to pour.

## Setup

The cellar is 30 by 20 feet with a 10-foot ceiling, brick walls, and barrels stacked along one side.
There are three features: the bolted trapdoor above, a heavy iron door in the north wall, and a rusted grate in the floor.
Water pours from a pipe high in the east wall. It rises 2 feet at the end of each round.
Round 3: the floor is difficult terrain. Round 4: Small creatures swim. Round 5: everyone swims. End of round 6: no air.
A creature without air takes the hazard damage at the start of each of its turns.

## Twist

The iron door is the obvious way out, and it is the wrong one. It holds back the canal.
Forcing it (Strength against the DC) floods the cellar in a single round: skip straight to round 5.
The grate is the way out. It leads to a storm drain wide enough to crawl.
Clearing it takes 4 successes against the DC, one attempt per creature per round: Strength to lift, thieves' tools on the pins, Intelligence (Investigation) to find the release, or anything clever. From round 3 the work is underwater and made with disadvantage unless the creature can breathe or see there.
The pipe can be plugged with a barrel lid and a Strength check against the DC: the water rises 1 foot a round instead.
The trapdoor is solid: three successes against the DC plus 5, and someone must be lifted to reach it.
Reveal: an Intelligence (Investigation) or Wisdom (Perception) check against the DC hears the canal slapping behind the iron door, and sees the damp line across it at head height.

## Terrain and tactics

Say the water height aloud at the end of every round.
Barrels float from round 3: something to stand on, or to plug the pipe.
There is no enemy in the room. Do not add one.

## Ways out without a fight

All of them. The room is the opponent.
Shouting to whoever bolted the trapdoor works only with real leverage: Charisma (Persuasion or Intimidation) against the DC plus 5.

## Ripple

Out through the drain: the party surfaces somewhere in the city they were not meant to be, unseen.
Out through the trapdoor: whoever bolted it knows they failed, and warns their employer.
Canal door opened: the street above floods, and every table's market is ankle-deep tomorrow.

## Reskin notes

A grain silo filling with wheat, a sealing tomb losing air, a ship's hold taking on water.
Decide who slid the bolt in your own notes, not here.
