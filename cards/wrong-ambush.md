## Hook

A felled tree blocks the forest road. "Purses on the ground, nice and slow," calls a voice from the bracken.

## Setup

The road is 15 feet wide between steep, wooded banks.
The robbers are spread along both banks with half cover. One guards a squirming sack behind the fallen tree.
They want coin, not blood. They attack if refused.
Run the first round as a plain ambush. The big creature in the roster is not here yet.

## Twist

The sack holds the young of something large. The robbers took it this morning to sell.
At the start of round 2 the parent arrives from the side of the road nobody is watching. It is the big creature in the roster.
It attacks whoever is nearest the sack, then whoever last hurt it. It does not care which side they are on.
The robbers panic: half of them shoot the creature, half run.
If the young is freed and unharmed, the parent takes it and leaves at once.
If the young is hurt, the parent fights to the death and has advantage on attack rolls.
Reveal: a Wisdom (Perception) check against the DC in round 1 hears the sack whimper, and something heavy answering in the trees.

## Terrain and tactics

The banks are difficult terrain to climb.
The fallen tree is three-quarters cover for anyone behind it, including the sack's guard.
Cutting the sack open takes an action.

## Ways out without a fight

Pay. They ask 10 gp a head and take half.
Tell them what is coming. If the party heard it, the robbers drop the sack and flee: no check.
Free the young before round 2 and step back.

## Ripple

Young returned: the creature's territory stays quiet, and the road is safe for other tables.
Young killed or sold: something large hunts this stretch of road in later episodes.
Robbers escaped with the party's coin: they are spending it in the next town, loudly.

## Reskin notes

The young can be a griffon chick, a prize hound, or a noble's kidnapped heir with bodyguards on the way.
In a city, the third party is the watch, arriving for the robbers and the party alike.
